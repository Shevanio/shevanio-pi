# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive project.
Contributors must communicate in good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and
respect differing experience, identity, and perspective.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct,
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing `shevanio-pi` in
public or private project interactions. Repository maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms
the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.
Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
